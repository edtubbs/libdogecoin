=== ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS ===

The TX_R format is correct and identical between mined and unconfirmed versions.
The relay/mining failure is NOT caused by a format or encoding regression.

Root cause: Dogecoin relay policy rejects non-standard P2SH redeem scripts.

  - The carrier redeem script OP_DROP OP_DROP OP_DROP OP_DROP OP_DROP OP_1
    (hex: 757575757551) is consensus-valid but NOT standard.
  - Dogecoin Core's MANDATORY_SCRIPT_VERIFY_FLAGS = SCRIPT_VERIFY_P2SH only.
    The OP_DROP*5 OP_1 script evaluates correctly under P2SH consensus rules.
  - However, IsStandardTx() / AreInputsStandard() reject non-standard
    scriptPubKey types. P2SH scripts that don't match known templates
    (P2PKH, P2SH-P2WPKH, multisig, etc.) are rejected at mempool policy level.
  - Most Dogecoin nodes will NOT relay TX_R to peers.

Why did the 3 old TX_R get mined?
  - All 3 were mined within a 67-minute window (21:03 - 22:10 UTC, April 7).
  - They likely reached a miner running with relaxed policy (e.g. -acceptnonstd=1)
    or were submitted directly to a mining pool API.
  - Once in a block, all nodes accept them (consensus-valid).

Why are the 2 new TX_R stuck?
  - They are visible on SoChain and Blockchair (limited mempool propagation).
  - They are NOT visible on BlockCypher (rejected by most nodes).
  - No miner with relaxed policy has picked them up yet.
  - Higher fee (500k koinu on c32635aa) does NOT help because rejection is
    policy-based (non-standard script type), not fee-based.

SPV cannot detect unconfirmed TX_R because SPV only processes transactions
within confirmed blocks. Until the TX_R are mined, SPV will continue showing:
  "TX_C OP_RETURN commitment found; waiting for TX_R carrier_scriptsig match in a later block..."

The SPV scanning for A6bAFnGqeKDiYk9dwkLqJSYX96ECHZ2f3q is working correctly.
It detects TX_C carrier outputs at that address and scans all block TX scriptSigs
for FLC1FULL carrier tags. The issue is that unconfirmed TX_R never appear in blocks.

=== RECOMMENDATIONS ===

1. DIRECT MINER SUBMISSION: Submit TX_R directly to mining pool APIs or nodes
   running with -acceptnonstd=1 to bypass relay policy rejection.

2. FEE GUIDANCE: Keep TX_R fee at 500,000 koinu (0.005 DOGE) as documented.
   While fee alone doesn't fix the non-standard rejection, higher fees may
   incentivize miners who DO accept non-standard TXs to prioritize them.

3. FUTURE: Phase 2 reserved-opcode upgrade will make PQC scripts standard,
   eliminating the relay policy issue entirely.

4. CARRIER OUTPUT VALUE: Maintain 1 DOGE (100,000,000 koinu) minimum to avoid
   dust rejection, which is a separate policy check.
